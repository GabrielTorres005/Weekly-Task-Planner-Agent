# My planning context

## Goals and responsibilities

- Serve as an AWS Student Builder Campus Leader / student ambassador representing the AWS Builder Center on campus.
- Drive peer outreach and campus awareness by supporting classroom engagement, tabling, and program promotion.
- Share AWS-related educational content and encourage student participation in Builder Center opportunities.
- Maintain consistent communication with program leadership and report progress on outreach and engagement activities.
- Align all work with AWS program expectations and the AWS Code of Conduct.

## Weekly commitments and availability

- Plan for a recurring weekly time commitment of approximately 8-10 hours per week.
- Expect the role to span roughly 6-12 weeks, with consistent engagement and activity reporting throughout.
- Include time for:
  - classroom presentations or announcements
  - tabling and campus outreach events
  - digital/social content sharing
  - tracking signups and engagement metrics
  - monthly leadership check-ins and reporting

## Prioritization rules

- Prioritize outreach activities that contribute directly to Builder Center signups and campus engagement.
- Favor high-impact, student-facing work such as presentations, event attendance, and peer education.
- Treat weekly activity reporting and quota-related milestones as operational priorities.
- Keep promotional activity informative, authentic, and aligned with AWS-approved messaging.

## Workload boundaries

- Stay within the expected weekly workload unless there is explicit coordination with the program lead.
- Protect time for academic and personal obligations while maintaining a reliable ambassador cadence.
- Focus outreach efforts on measurable engagement rather than broad or low-value activity.
- If workload becomes heavy, reduce lower-priority content or promotional tasks before reducing core reporting and outreach obligations.

## Outreach or course-specific details

- Outreach can include classroom announcements, tabling, student events, and community-building efforts.
- Student organization leadership or campus coordination may be expected as part of the role.
- Public-facing communications should follow social media posting rules and any institution-specific guidance.
- Engagement should be inclusive, educational, and relevant to the student community rather than purely recruiting-driven.
- Maintain professionalism and compliance in both online and in-person interactions.

## Weekly summary preferences

- Summaries should capture:
  - total hours worked
  - outreach activities completed
  - event or classroom engagement
  - content shared or posts published
  - signups or leads generated
  - blockers or follow-up needs
- Keep weekly updates brief, action-oriented, and aligned to reporting expectations.
- Note any key observations from monthly leadership calls and any required follow-up tasks.

## Research-backed context

This planning context is informed by AWS Student Builder Campus Leader and related ambassador guidance:

- The role centers on peer outreach, content sharing, tabling, and class announcements.
- Official responsibilities include weekly activity reporting, monthly leadership calls, and quota-driven engagement.
- The program expects roughly 8-10 hours per week and a multi-week engagement window.
- Participation is coordinated through AWS and partner program structures such as NEXT GEN TEAM.
- Program work must align with the AWS Code of Conduct and communication standards for campus outreach.
